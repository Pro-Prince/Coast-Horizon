Santa Monica

A freely drivable district adapted from the owner’s Santa Monica export (topoexport-FD8522.zip), at real metre scale. All exported streets remain accessible; the world ends at the survey boundary. Building details and roadway widths are simplified for gameplay. This is not a navigation or survey map.

Buildings and roads: Overture Maps Foundation (2026), ODbL. Trees and green areas: Overture Maps Foundation Base (2026), ODbL; canopy heights: Meta and World Resources Institute Global Canopy Height Maps v2 (2026), CC BY 4.0. Terrain: USGS 3D Elevation Program (2026), US Government Public Domain. Export provided by TopoExport.

Modified geographic data is available under ODbL 1.0: optimized 3D district, tree positions, street network and terrain, conversion metadata. See the original export notices.

Surface textures and reflection lighting: Poly Haven, CC0. See material sources and credits. Foliage and window textures are original procedural artwork.
